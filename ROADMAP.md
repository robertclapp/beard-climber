# Beard Climber Roadmap

Pip from Whiskerton climbs the beards of grumpy giants to return what they lost and cheer them up. Each world ends with a boss giant, and the difficulty ramps up as you go. Easy mode is available for younger players.

## How each world is built
- One world per batch, each with its own art pack loaded on demand.
- Every giant gets an intro (Pip packs the lost item while townspeople cheer), a unique mechanic, a shop set, collectibles and an ending.
- After each giant there is a parachute coin run, then a bird carries Pip to the next giant.
- The difficulty is tuned so bosses are a real step up.
